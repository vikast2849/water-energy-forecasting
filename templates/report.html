"Population-Driven Forecasting: A Machine Learning Framework for Urban & Rural Water & Energy Demand"

1. Executive Overview & Problem Statement

Rapid urbanization and population growth have created unprecedented challenges in managing essential municipal resources such as water and energy. Urban centers face rising consumption due to industrialization and lifestyle factors, while rural regions struggle with resource scarcity and seasonal agricultural vulnerability. Existing forecasting models often treat urban and rural contexts uniformly, leading to severe resource misallocation, municipal water wastage in urban hubs, and acute power deficits in farming clusters.

This project develops an integrated, population-driven machine learning framework combining demographic metrics, climate variables (temperature, rainfall), and economic indices to forecast both continuous demand (MLD for water, MWh for energy) and categorical scarcity risk alerts.

2. Machine Learning Architecture (SVM, KNN, Logistic Regression)

As outlined in Chapter 2 & 4 of the project syllabus, three foundational machine learning algorithms were engineered to achieve a balance between accuracy, interpretability, and computational efficiency:

  • Support Vector Machine (SVM / SVR): Employs a non-linear Radial Basis Function (RBF) kernel to map high-dimensional demographic and climate variables, capturing complex urban non-linear demand patterns.
  • K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN): Implements distance-weighted neighborhood aggregation (k=5) to model localized, community-specific rural demand where historical consumption mirrors regional farming clusters.
  • Logistic Regression: Provides an interpretable, sigmoid-based probabilistic classification ($P(\text{Scarcity}) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-z}}$) to detect severe drought or peak load conditions with transparent log-odds feature coefficients.

4. Utility Decision Support & Policy Guidelines

Based on the comparative findings and predictive thresholds, municipal utility boards (e.g., KUWSDB and BESCOM/MESCOM) can enact the following data-driven interventions:

  1. Urban Water Conservation: Mitigate the {{ kpis.water_disparity_ratio }}x per-capita disparity by enforcing smart greywater recycling for high-density apartments and commercial zones.
  2. Rural Agricultural Feeder Staggering: During high scarcity summer periods, schedule 3-phase agricultural power distribution in rotating night shifts to prevent substation overload while ensuring fair irrigation access.
  3. Solar Micro-Irrigation Promotion: In semi-arid taluks like Challakere and Molakalmuru, transition unmetered agricultural pumps to solar-powered drip irrigation to cushion the dual water-energy shock.
